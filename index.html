    Integration status

    No supported bed or sensor models or communication protocol have been configured. Pairing to an arbitrary Bluetooth device would not establish a safe bed integration.

    • Document manufacturer-approved bed and pressure-sensor models and firmware.
    • Obtain the vendor protocol: transport, service/characteristic identifiers, data formats, authentication, and security requirements.
    • Validate units, calibration, patient/device association, timestamps, freshness thresholds, and disconnect behavior.
    • Define patient-specific authorization, contraindication checks, command bounds, stop behavior, and explicit device acknowledgment before enabling controls.
    • Complete clinical, privacy, accessibility, and safety validation before use with patients.
